## THE HOOK (40 Seconds)

*(Start here — this is your first 40 seconds)*

"In 2022, I was 15, learning web development in my free time when I was in school. I joined a WhatsApp group called 'website buyers' where people trade monetized websites. That's where I met Farhan — he'd just started his own branded saree business. We became friends. I'd help him optimize his Shopify store, rank on Google.

Then, one night at midnight, my phone rang. It was Farhan — crying.

Earlier that evening, he'd gone to his close friend Aisha's birthday. One of Aisha's friends was wearing a saree from Farhan's brand. Farhan asked her excitedly: 'Where did you get that?'

She said: 'Amazon. I found it through chatting with ChatGPT.'

Farhan told her: 'That's my brand — I sell on Amazon too!'

She just shrugged and said: 'I don't know. I just get everything from Amazon.'

He was devastated. He's been building his brand for years. He's even planning to get his own manufacturing factory. But his own customers don't know his brand exists. They think they're just buying from Amazon.

That's when I realized: **If your brand isn't visible to AI, you don't exist to the next generation of shoppers.**

So I built payally."

## S — STAGE (Set the Scene)

**The world before AI shopping:**

Farhan is like millions of SMB brands. He sells on Shopify AND Amazon. His products are good — people buy them. But here's what happens:

- Customer asks ChatGPT: "Where can I buy silk sarees?"
- ChatGPT recommends products — from Amazon, from giant retailers
- Farhan's brand? Invisible. Not in the recommendation loop.
- Even customers WHO BOUGHT his products don't know his brand name

This isn't Farhan's fault. It's a structural shift in how shopping works.

## T — TENSION (The Pain)

**The problem is getting worse:**

- 50% of Google searches will have AI summaries by 2028 (McKinsey)
- Brand websites supply only **1%** of sources AI assistants cite (McKinsey)
- AI-driven referral traffic to retail: **+693%** during holiday 2025 (McKinsey)
- 29% of AI shoppers say AI chat had biggest influence on their last purchase (McKinsey)

**The painful truth:**

Big tech is building agentic commerce — but ONLY for enterprise:
- PayPal's Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) — enterprise pilots only
- Google's Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) — Walmart, Target partners
- Custom development — $100K+, 6+ months, 93% exceed budgets (McKinsey)

**SMBs ($1M-$20M revenue) have ZERO options.**

Farhan can't afford $100K. He doesn't have an engineering team. He's being left behind by the AI shopping shift — while his products still sell on Amazon.
